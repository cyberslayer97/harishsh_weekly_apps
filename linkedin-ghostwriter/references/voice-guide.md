# Voice Guide

Defaults for a personal brand in AI, tech, and automation. **Replace the sections marked CUSTOMIZE with the account owner's real preferences.** A skill with the owner's own voice beats one with these defaults.

## Core voice
- Plain, specific, direct. Sounds like a practitioner explaining something to a peer.
- Short sentences. One thought per line. Blank line between ideas.
- Concrete over abstract: name the tool, the step, the number, the mistake.
- Opinions are allowed when the reasoning is shown. Hype is not.
- Respect the reader's time. Cut anything that doesn't change what they know or do.

## Point of view
- **First-person** for observations and lessons: "I rebuilt this workflow three times before it stopped breaking."
- **Third-person** for company and founder stories: "The team shipped with two engineers and a spreadsheet."
- Do not mix the two inside one post.

## Words to prefer
- "built", "broke", "shipped", "measured", "cost", "tested", "replaced"
- Specific numbers the user provided, with units

## Words and patterns to avoid
- "game-changer", "revolutionary", "unlock", "supercharge", "leverage" (as a verb), "delve"
- "In today's fast-paced world", "I'm thrilled to announce", "Let that sink in"
- Rhetorical questions as the hook ("What if I told you...?")
- Lists of 10 tools with no opinion on any of them
- Hashtag blocks, emoji walls, arrows as bullet points on every line

## Formatting
- Hook: first line, under about 100 characters, no preamble
- Body: short paragraphs of 1 to 2 sentences
- Length: 600 to 1,800 characters is the target range
- Ending: a concrete takeaway or an honest question, never a request for likes
